\# Employee Leave Policy



\## Annual Leave



Employees get 24 days of annual leave per year.



Leave should normally be requested at least 3 days in advance.



\## Sick Leave



Employees get 12 days of sick leave per year.



Employees should inform their manager when they are sick.



\## Bereavement Leave



Employees can take 5 working days of bereavement leave.



\## Leave Requests



Employees should submit leave requests through the HR system.



Access: HR
