\# Employee Benefits



\## Health Insurance



Eligible employees receive company health insurance.



Coverage includes the employee, spouse, and dependent children.



\## Retirement



The company contributes 5% of eligible base salary to the retirement plan.



\## Wellness



Employees receive a wellness allowance of Rs. 10,000 per year.



\## Learning



Employees can request reimbursement for approved training and certifications.



Access: HR
