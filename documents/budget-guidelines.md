\# Budget Guidelines



\## Budget Rules



Departments must stay within their approved budgets.



\## Hiring



New hires must have an approved headcount request.



\## Software



Software purchases should be approved before purchase.



\## Travel



Business travel must be reasonable and related to company work.



\## Variance



A budget difference of more than 10% should be reviewed by Finance.



\## Unplanned Expenses



Large unplanned expenses should be discussed with Finance before spending.
